# Venue Outreach — Cold Booking Enquiry

**Purpose:** first-contact email to a venue's booker / promoter to enquire about a DJ set.

**How to use:** copy the body below into your mail client, replace every `[BRACKETED]` field, delete this instruction block, send. Keep the tone in mind — house music venues get generic pitches all week; personal beats polished.

---

**To:** [BOOKER EMAIL]
**Subject:** Booking enquiry — Velvet Vibrance (house DJ)

---

Hi [BOOKER FIRST NAME],

I'm Glenn — the DJ behind Velvet Vibrance, a house music project based in [YOUR CITY].

I've been paying attention to what you're doing at [VENUE NAME] — [ONE SPECIFIC THING: a night you run, a recent booking that caught your ear, the room's sound, the residents]. Felt like there might be a fit, so wanted to introduce myself.

Quick sense of what I bring:
- **Sound:** [deep / afro / progressive / melodic] house — [ONE-LINE FEEL: "warm, groove-first, builds slow"]
- **Sets:** [SET LENGTH]-hour, comfortable in warm-up, peak, or closing slots
- **Format:** [USBs / vinyl / hybrid], [CDJ-3000s / XDJ / turntables] all fine
- **Recent mix:** [MIX LINK — SoundCloud / Mixcloud / YouTube]
- **Full presence:** velvet-vibrance.com · [INSTAGRAM HANDLE]

I'm open for dates across [MONTH RANGE], and happy to work around what you've already got booked — support slots, opening, back-to-backs, whatever fits.

Would love to have a proper chat if there's an opening. Happy to send more mixes, references, or come by for a night to introduce myself in person.

Cheers,
Glenn Gallacher
Velvet Vibrance

## Notes for future you

- **Always personalise the "one specific thing" line.** Bookers can smell a template from the subject line; the venue-specific sentence is what makes them read the rest.
- **Attach nothing on first contact.** Links only. Attachments trip spam filters and feel presumptuous.
- **Send Tuesday–Thursday, 10am–2pm venue local time.** Weekends and Mondays get buried.
- **Follow-up rule:** one polite nudge after 10 days, then move on. Do not chase further — a "no" or silence is data, use it to refine the pitch for the next venue.
- **Track every send in the Sheet** (`Gigs` tab → status: `inquired`). If they reply, update status.
