# Post-Gig Thank-You Email + Invoice

**Purpose:** send within 24 hours of playing a gig. Warm, professional, keeps the door open for a return booking, and delivers the invoice cleanly.

**How to use:** copy the body below, replace every `[BRACKETED]` field, attach the invoice PDF (generated from `templates/invoice.html`), send. Delete this instruction block first.

---

**To:** [BOOKER EMAIL]
**Subject:** Thank you — Velvet Vibrance @ [VENUE], [DATE]
**Attachment:** VV-invoice-[NNNN].pdf

---

Hi [BOOKER FIRST NAME],

Thanks for having me at [VENUE NAME] on [DATE] — genuinely one of the highlights of my month.

The [ROOM / SOUND SYSTEM / CROWD] was excellent, and the energy through [SPECIFIC MOMENT: "the last hour", "the peak-time transition into X track", "the closing set"] made it a night I won't forget. Massive thanks to [SPECIFIC PERSON: sound engineer / door team / green room / other DJ on the bill if it applies] as well — they made the whole thing run smoothly.

Invoice attached for the [DATE] set. Payment details are on the PDF — happy to send bank details another way if you'd prefer, or invoice through a different system if that's easier on your side.

I'd love to come back. Whenever you're programming [NEXT SEASON / MONTH / RESIDENCY / SPECIAL], let me know and I'll block out dates around it. Also happy to bring [SUPPORT / GUEST / B2B PROPOSAL] to the table for future bookings if that's ever of interest.

Really appreciate you giving me the room. Talk soon.

Cheers,
[YOUR NAME]
Velvet Vibrance
[YOUR EMAIL] · [YOUR PHONE]
velvet-vibrance.com

---

## Notes for future you

- **24-hour rule.** Send this the day after the gig, not the same night (you'll be tired and it'll show), not three days later (too cool).
- **Specific moments beat generic praise.** "The peak-time transition" reads real; "great night" reads copy-paste.
- **Never chase payment in this email.** The invoice does that job. If payment isn't received by the due date, send a separate polite reminder a week after the due date.
- **Update the Sheet.** `Gigs` tab → status: `played`, add fee. `Income` tab → new row for the invoice amount, category `gig`, link back to gig.
